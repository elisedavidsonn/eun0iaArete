This "blog" is dedicated to the biographies of individuals I have researched. I strongly believe that each story is unfinished, even within the excess of effort...no individual can be entirely capture without the personal word, and even then, our memories are subject to change. I encourage all biographies to be read with a similar disposition; unfinished are our lives in the histories of our existence.
