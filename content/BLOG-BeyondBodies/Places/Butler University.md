My not-so-humble undergraduate alumni. 
## Irwin Library
I was fortunate enough to work in the dark and cold corners of Irwin, AKA the “Digital Scholarship Lab,” alongside the fantastic [Julian Cabiya](https://libguides.butler.edu/prf.php?id=6208621e-7cdb-11ed-9922-0ad758b798c3), both my mentor and close-friend. For two years (2024-2026), I was the sole Digital AV Archivist for the [Visiting Writers Series](https://www.butler.edu/arts-sciences/english/visiting-writers/) at Butler University, hosted by the [English Department](https://www.butler.edu/arts-sciences/english/faculty-staff/) and the [Special Collections & Archives](https://libguides.butler.edu/SpecialCollections/Collections/UniversityArchives).
