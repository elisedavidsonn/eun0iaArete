## Irwin Library
I worked in the dark and cold corners of the Library AKA the “Digital Scholarship Lab” alongside the fantastic Julian Cabiya, both my mentor and close-friend. For two years, I was the sole-digital archivist for the Visiting Writers Series at Butler University, hosted by the [English Department](https://www.butler.edu/arts-sciences/english/faculty-staff/) and the [Special Collections & Archives](https://libguides.butler.edu/SpecialCollections/Collections/UniversityArchives).
