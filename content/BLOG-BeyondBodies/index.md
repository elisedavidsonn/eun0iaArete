# Welcome to Beyond Bodies! 

This blog space is dedicated to the biographies of individuals I have personally researched. I strongly believe that each story is unfinished, even within the excess of effort. No individual can be entirely captured without personal word, and even then, memory is subject to change with time lived. I encourage all stories to be read with a similar disposition; unfinished are our lives in the histories of our existence.
