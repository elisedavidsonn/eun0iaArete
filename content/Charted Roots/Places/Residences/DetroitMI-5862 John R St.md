Named by **John R. Williams** (1782-1852), the first elected major of Detroit.
* Originally two parallel streets referenced his namesake: "**John R. Street**" and "**Williams Street**" in downtown Detroit.
* In 1890, a street on the westside, **Williams Avenue,** was constructed, then renamed Williams Street. The original **Williams Street** was renamed to **Witherell Street** (after Supreme Court Justice Benjamin F.H. Witherell).
