## Detroit Street Names
- ["Eleven Detroit Streets Named After Slaveowners"](https://www.blac.media/news-features/eleven-detroit-streets-named-after-slaveowners/) @ 2017-02-01
	1. Joseph Campau (1769-1863) = "Joseph Campau Street"
		1. *"Barons of Detroit"* - original settler's family
		2. <u>Uncle</u> of John R. Williams
	2. Lewis Cass (1782-1866) 
		1. "Cass Avenue" > The Cass Corridor > Midtown Detroit
		2. Runs parallel to M-1 (Woodward Avenue) 
	3. William Macomb (1751-1796) = "Macomb Street"
		1. Moved to Detroit for Phyn and Ellice in 1774. 
		2. The largest slaveholder in Michigan at time of death
	4. John R. Williams (1782-1852) = "John R. Street" / "Williams Avenue"
	5. Jean Baptiste Beaubien (1787-1864) = "Beaubien Street" > "St. Antoine Street"
	6. Elijah Brush (1774-1823) = Brush Park
	7. "Edmund Street"
		1. "Alfred Street"
		2. "Adelaide Street"
		3. "Brush Street"
	8. George McDougall = "McDougall Street" & [[Belle Isle]]
		1. [Belle Isle & 1943 Detroit Race Riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1943_Detroit_race_riot) 
	9. Chene Park & "Chene Street" > [renamed after Aretha Franklin (2018)](https://michiganchronicle.com/detroit-park-named-after-former-slave-owners-to-be-renamed-after-aretha-franklin/)
	10. Thomas Jefferson = "Jefferson Avenue"
	11. "Rivard Street"
		1. Family owned a ribbon farm 
	12. George Washington = "Washington Boulevard"
