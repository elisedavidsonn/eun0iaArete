# Welcome!

My name is Eli(se) Davidson. I am an aspiring local archivist, genealogist, and storyteller with B.A.s in Anthropology and English Literary Theory, located in the Midwest. Much of my research has been personally fueled by a passion to comprehend this life through the minds of many. I spend many hours between various networks investigating the often unspoken connections that make our lives all the more human. 

Adamant about our Nation's histories of colonial and imperialist foundations, I believe we must arise each day to acknowledge our ancestors and descendants to which we owe this country; combined with the daily efforts of existence, how are we to manage? 

Beyond the past, I spend my time in the present; seeking ways to encounter and efface the change we need to see in this world. If you find yourself aligned, please reach out. In brief, many of my personal beliefs align with adrienne maree brown's [[emergent strategies]] (2017); see [[Philosophies]] for a more in-depth investigation into my guiding thoughts.
