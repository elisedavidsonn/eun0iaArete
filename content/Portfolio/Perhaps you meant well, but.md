each time I stop and sit with you I leave
weary –stained with guilt and empty–
handed. Dad says: it will grow and blossom
and change, and you and I will feel different.
but I am only a daughter in need of a
mother because my skin feels too tight
and you offer me another almond.

I could never talk to you Mom, but you confided in me.
You never taught me more than dirty
words and things I would never feel because
who could love a mother’s daughter who hates herself.
_she is more than, am I bound to be less?_

Beyond the confines of my mother’s curves and
my father’s square shoulders, you showed me
that I will always be seen.
So, perhaps I’ll kiss my parter
sway her in my arms
Like I meant to long ago but never had
the words and what has been has been
Now, I abide to silence; weary, with guilt
and empty handed because \[**redacted**]
