in my window.
She cannot speak without
smiling, in discontent at
every compliment I
throw at her. Often,
her thumbs dance on
my sill and the crisp wind
tells me to turn off the lights.

There is a woman
in my window.
She likes to remind me
to return back to,
maybe retreat,
to the bed, where
she and I are safe again.

The window in the woman
whispers of a life so simple,
no need to reach byeond
definitions and titles
because she just is
yet I am not, because
what once was hers is no longer.

I cannot speak without
drawing the blinds.
