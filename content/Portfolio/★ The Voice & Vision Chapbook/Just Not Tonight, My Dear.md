you ask whether this gold cage is really a home,
but no one gives an ear, so you eat
another feather than see it fall because
the marble floor is so close and too far
and you can’t bear to test the lock beyond
that initial mordant click.

to want more is to ask less, so you eat
another feather because your mouth feels like
a graveyard where words wither on your tongue.

speak. speak. speak.

you await in silence, in cold and bare
glimmers of gold will strike you, mayhap
the Sun aligns and for an instance you will fly,
fly for a home, but nothing more because
you are full of yourself.
