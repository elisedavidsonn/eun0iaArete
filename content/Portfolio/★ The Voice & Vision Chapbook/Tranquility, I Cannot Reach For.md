Amber, like the pawn of the
night that I steal in
my pockets and deep consumption
of dirty pennies, dirty lint, and dirty
thoughts of deluded warmth
I thought she might bless
but she’s different than my mirror
and her eyes burn dim.

A sweet savory orange with
delectable specks of depravity.

Oh Amber,

why does she float alone?
A small-town bar
reflecting on window-less pains
plausibly disrupting imaginative
desire that burns far-off
in the depths of the coals
where her father returns
petrified each morning.

May she fossilize my eyes
so decorative and ornate
with the fervor of deceit and
the red she aims at her mother.

But each night,
she reaches to the rising sun
to greet Her.
Maybe, a soft glow stretches
across the heat of her coffee
and you’ll dream of likeness in Her
simper smile and in her laugh.

No longer do you find joy, Amber.

No longer do you sleep
on my doorstep, Amber.

Do you wish to leave?
Do I know how to say goodbye?
please, let the warmth
seep through my skin smoothly,
for my mind runs circles
and sets pace that even her
vibrancy cannot blind
My competition.

So seek once more,
another glance of sweet, savory orange,
but find no comfort
in the mornings when her windows
no longer shine of Amber.
