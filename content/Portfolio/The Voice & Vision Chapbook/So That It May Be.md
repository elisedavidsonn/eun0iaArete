And I glance at you with such grimace and disgrace
and disgust that I can only find in 
reflections of the neo-modernism you so desperately
wish to evade, but what good would be our
absence, if not one of contempt and solitude that
a Being would expect in its social nature. 

May my eyes penetrate the glass corridors you have built
beyond your harmless intuition. Did you really
think of yourself as a threat? Each Fraktur remains
so dense in our mind as you scramble to rearrange
the fragments of your own memorial. May my blue
grant you serenity from a sonorous World, 
and mark my words of truth and sincerity
while you float along the cosmic freeway,
our final destination. 

**April 11, 2023**
