Yet, in endless moments
of empathetic mourning
You ask me: when what where _more_ can I be?

Can I slide my blade
through the throat of the
Hydra, my love.

Can I release the pollen
that rebukes the breath
You must take.

You have fought long enough.
Days of your mother and your grandmother
for which You exist(ed), beyond
the philosophical boundaries,
science is just like mine.

Clear Skies await, my love, for an antiquity
life You and you, you, and you, so may
pease find your temples, slow the ache,
and bless the sons and songs that follow.
