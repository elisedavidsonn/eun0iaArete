if you were to ask me when the clouds
had part for the sun to sneak into your sweater,
i would answer you      “i do not know
maybe the puddle from last April
finally sank into December and
i couldn’t get warm again and 
you couldn’t get home.” 

“then what happened”      you ask
and i will not answer, but you insist
so i say: 

“i forgot what i had for dinner
but i knew when you wore that last week
and i knew when you cut your hair
and i knew when you
and i knew you”

i think that your sweater
got too small

you purse your lips
sign a million signs
you say you think it is just me.

**March 27, 2023**
A part of the [[Portfolio/The Voice & Vision Chapbook/index|The Voice & Vision Chapbook]]; originally written for a mixed-media composition titled “One Half-Minute Video to Tell You I Exist.”
