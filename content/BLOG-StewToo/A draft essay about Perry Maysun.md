As a young *girl* who avoided every idea of a “parasocial relationship” during the height of One Direction, I find myself ever encroaching on the sheer honesty and talent demonstrated by the [BYOBF artist group](https://www.byobf.com/about). The entire collective resolves around the immense bonds between: 
- Perry Maysun, AKA Calvin Maysun-Pink (once, YungJuice)
- ZionKat
- MarikaSage 
- IsaiahPoet
- AhkSair
- Amouri
- IndigoMak
- Yo OG

BYOBF stands for “Be Your Own Best Friend,” an acronym first worn by Perry Maysun, arguably the group’s current lead image. However, saying that overlooks the backend production work done by the experimental ZionKat; he’s produced over 7/8th of Perry’s track lists and touts his own impressive, mid-city, sci-fi, yearner-type beats and lyricism.
