As a young girl who avoided every idea of a “parasocial relationship” during the height of *One Direction*, I find myself ever encroaching upon the sheer honesty and talent demonstrated by the [BYOBF artist group](https://www.byobf.com/about). The entire collective resolves around the immense bonds between: 
- Perry Maysun, AKA Calvin Maysun-Pink (once, YungJuice)
- ZionKat
- MarikaSage 
- IsaiahPoet
- AhkSair
- Amouri
- IndigoMak
- Yo OG

BYOBF stands for “Be Your Own Best Friend,” an acronym first worn by Perry Maysun, arguably the group’s current lead image. However, saying that overlooks the backend production work done by the experimental ZionKat. He’s produced over 7/8th of Perry’s track lists and touts his own impressive, mid-city, sci-fi, yearner-type beats and lyricism. Each artist proclaims their own identity in every track; it truly is a wonder to see such dedication, creativity, and friendship extend across the nation. 

Marika Sage is an experimental rap and hip-hip artist who delves into queer relationships, self-shame, and addiction with a truly, (mind the pun) addictive, penmanship. Isaiah Poet is likely to reach to the collective, applying the societal restrictions to our real-world afflictions, while Ahk Sair feeds the underground collective, expressing deeply-felt emotional constraints placed to an ephemeral Being; Zion Kat’s personal mixtapes evoke a very similar feelings. Amouri, whose name means love, is an extraordinary individual who bases their work on the shared love between existence, more particularly, the beauty in the borderline of Being “boring.” Indigo Mak shows the same sentiments, accompanying lo-fi, soft rasp-ness to personal encounters. Yo OG calls about the production of the self with albums co-produced alongside Ja’King the Divine. 

All together, the BYOBF Collective is an inside-look into what it means to be a productive Human in the creative space during the Digital Age: whether viewed as a sale of the soul or beyond, these individuals have shown extreme humility and empathy within their own endeavors. I personally wish them all the best, having met a majority of the Collective in Chicago on Juneteenth 2026 during the Pathetic Beasts Tour.
